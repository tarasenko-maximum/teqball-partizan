What is Teqball?

Teqball is a hybrid sport that combines football and table tennis. It is played on a curved table (teq table) that allows the ball to bounce back to the player, creating a dynamic and fast game.

The sport was invented by Gábor Borsányi in 2014 in Hungary. Today it is governed by FITEQ (International Teqball Federation) with over 114 national federations worldwide.

Teqball is recognized by GAISF and aims for inclusion in the Olympic Games by 2028.

⚽ Match is best of 3 sets, each set to 12 points
🚫 No hands allowed
👆 Maximum 3 touches before the ball crosses the net
🔄 No body part may touch the ball twice consecutively
