About Us

Our Story

Teqball Partizan is the official teqball section of JSD Partizan, established with the goal of promoting and developing the fastest growing sport in the world in Serbia.

Teqball was created in 2014 in Hungary, and thanks to the exceptional results of Serbian players on the international stage, it has become one of the most promising sports in the region.

Our players are multiple world and European champions. Nikola Mitro and Bogdan Marojević won world gold in 2018, 2019, 2021, and 2022, while Maja Umićević won silver at the 2023 European Games in Krakow.

Under the auspices of JSD Partizan, at Humska 1 in Belgrade, our team trains and prepares for the world's biggest competitions. We are members of the Teqball Federation of Serbia (SRBTEQ) and actively cooperate with FITEQ, the world teqball federation.

Our mission is to make teqball a recognized sport in Serbia, inspire young people to take up this dynamic sport, and continue achieving success on the international stage.
